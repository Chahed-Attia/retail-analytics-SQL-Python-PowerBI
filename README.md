# retail-analytics-SQL-Python-PowerBI
End-to-end Data Analytics portfolio project analyzing customer shopping trends from retail data using SQL, Python, and Power BI.
# Problem statement 
A leading retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior in order to improve sales, customer satisfaction, and a long-term loyalty. The management team has noticed changes in purchasing patterns across demographics, product categories, and sales channels (online vs offline). They are particularly interested in uncovering which factors, such as discounts, reviews, seasons, or payement preferences, drive consumer decisions and repezt purchases.
# Task
Analyzing the company's consumer behavior dataset to answer the following business questions:" How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies? "
